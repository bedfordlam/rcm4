# RCM Level 4 Piano Practice

A daily practice routine for the RCM Level 4 piano exam: key of the day, metronome, ear trainer, sight-reading generator, technical-test tracker and a mock exam scoresheet. Everything is in `index.html`; progress is saved in your own browser.

Unofficial study aid, not affiliated with The Royal Conservatory. Requirements follow the RCM Piano Syllabus, 2022 Edition; confirm details with your teacher.

Piano recordings: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0.
